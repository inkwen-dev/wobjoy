# Wobjoy

![Wobjoy](assets/brand/wordmark.svg)

[English](README.md) · [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md)

Free browser games to play without downloading an app.

[Play on Wobjoy](https://wobjoy.com/)

## Featured games

### Egg Bonk

[![Egg Bonk](https://wobjoy.com/games/egg-bonk/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/egg-bonk/)

Pick an egg, use lucky stickers, and challenge nine opponents.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/egg-bonk/) · [How to play](games/en/egg-bonk.md)

### Tower of Hanoi

[![Tower of Hanoi](https://wobjoy.com/games/hanoi/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/tower-of-hanoi/)

Move the rings across three pegs in six classic Tower of Hanoi levels.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/tower-of-hanoi/) · [How to play](games/en/hanoi.md)

### Sokoban

[![Sokoban](https://wobjoy.com/games/sokoban/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/sokoban/)

Push every box onto a goal to clear the puzzle.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/sokoban/) · [How to play](games/en/sokoban.md)

### Shikaku

[![Shikaku](https://wobjoy.com/games/shikaku/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/shikaku/)

Use the numbers to divide the board into rectangles.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/shikaku/) · [How to play](games/en/shikaku.md)

### Trap the Sheep

[![Trap the Sheep](https://wobjoy.com/games/trapsheep/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/trapsheep/)

Move the wolves to trap the sheep before it escapes.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/trapsheep/) · [How to play](games/en/trapsheep.md)

### Classic Fruit Machine

[![Classic Fruit Machine](https://wobjoy.com/games/fruit-machine/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/fruit-machine/)

Start with 200 virtual coins and play up to 30 spins. Collect, double, or finish.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/fruit-machine/) · [How to play](games/en/fruit-machine.md)

### Crack for a Prize

[![Crack for a Prize](https://wobjoy.com/games/hunt-grid/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/crack-for-a-prize/)

Find hidden gifts by cracking eggs across six boards. Empty eggs reveal neighboring clues.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/crack-for-a-prize/) · [How to play](games/en/hunt-grid.md)

### Balance Master

[![Balance Master](https://wobjoy.com/games/balance-master/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/balance-master/)

Place ten weights without tipping the scale. Choose a weight and a tray; clear nine levels.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/balance-master/) · [How to play](games/en/balance-master.md)

### 2048

[![2048](https://wobjoy.com/games/n2048/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/2048/)

Swipe or use arrow keys to merge equal tiles and reach 2048.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/2048/) · [How to play](games/en/n2048.md)

### Derby Tactics

[![Derby Tactics](https://wobjoy.com/games/derby-tactics/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/derby-tactics/)

Read the rival lineup and choose one horse per race. Higher numbers win; plan which races to sacrifice.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/derby-tactics/) · [How to play](games/en/derby-tactics.md)

### Duskward

[![Duskward](https://wobjoy.com/games/duskward/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/duskward/)

Explore a rotating 3D space garden. Choose Step In or Tour. Requires WebGPU; there is no score or win condition.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/duskward/) · [How to play](games/en/duskward.md)

### Gustwing



Tap, click, or press Space to flap through gaps. Original game menus are in English; Wobjoy provides bilingual help.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/gustwing/) · [How to play](games/en/gustwing.md)

### Klarbase

[![Klarbase](https://wobjoy.com/games/klarbase/cover.webp)](https://wobjoy.com/games/klarbase/)

Guide Pip through 56 floating-island puzzles. Push crates, ride lifts and catch the wind.

[Play now](https://wobjoy.com/games/klarbase/) · [How to play](games/en/klarbase.md)
