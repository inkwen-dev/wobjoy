[English](../en/sokoban.md) · [简体中文](../zh-CN/sokoban.md)

![Sokoban](https://wobjoy.com/games/sokoban/cover.webp)

Push every box onto a goal to clear the puzzle.

## How to play

Move with the arrow keys or WASD, swipe, or tap an adjacent tile. Push one box at a time onto a goal. Boxes cannot be pulled. Undo a mistake or restart if a box gets stuck.
