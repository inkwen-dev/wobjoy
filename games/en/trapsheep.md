# Trap the Sheep

[English](../en/trapsheep.md) · [简体中文](../zh-CN/trapsheep.md)

![Trap the Sheep](https://wobjoy.com/games/trapsheep/cover.webp)

Move the wolves to trap the sheep before it escapes.

## How to play

Select or drag a wolf to an empty connected node. Wolves can move sideways or farther from their starting gate, but cannot move back toward it. Trap the sheep before it reaches an open gate. You also lose if the wolves run out of moves.
