# Tower of Hanoi

[English](../en/hanoi.md) · [简体中文](../zh-CN/hanoi.md)

![Tower of Hanoi](https://wobjoy.com/games/hanoi/cover.webp)

Move the rings across three pegs in six classic Tower of Hanoi levels.

## How to play

Click or tap a peg to pick up its top ring, then select another peg to place it. Move all rings to peg C. A larger ring cannot sit on a smaller ring. Try to finish with fewer moves.
