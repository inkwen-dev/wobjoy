# Shikaku

[English](../en/shikaku.md) · [简体中文](../zh-CN/shikaku.md)

![Shikaku](https://wobjoy.com/games/shikaku/cover.webp)

Use the numbers to divide the board into rectangles.

## How to play

Drag across the grid or tap two opposite corners to place a rectangle. Each rectangle must contain one number matching its area. Fill the board without gaps or overlaps. Tap a rectangle to remove it. Keyboard: arrow keys and Enter.
